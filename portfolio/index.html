01 / ABOUT

Learning the biology.
Building the computation.

My work sits at the intersection of biological questions, biological data, algorithms, software, validation, and interpretation.

I build projects that help me practice both sides of the field: understanding the biological problem and turning it into something computationally testable.

I deliberately distinguish between implemented, tested, simulated, and conceptual work rather than presenting a prototype as research proof.
